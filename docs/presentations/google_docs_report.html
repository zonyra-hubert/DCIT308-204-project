2. Problem Statement, Assumptions, Input-Output & System Boundaries

The Physical Development Directorate manages hundreds of daily facilities maintenance tickets across 200 physical locations and 200 road segments. Key challenges include road pavement degradation, shift budget constraints, and life-safety emergency overrides.
